About GoLive Musician’s League

GoLive grew out of Weekend Jam, founded in late 2023 by friends who love singing, playing instruments, and sharing the joy of performing. It has since become a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in the United States.

🎼

Open stages

Through events like Weekend Jam, we provide open stages for musicians of every level.

🤝

Artistic exchange

We encourage collaboration and exchange between singers, players and emerging bands.

🏠

A welcoming space

Community members who share a passion for music gather to connect, share, and create.

Instagram YouTube Xiaohongshu

Volunteer-run. Donor-powered.

GoLive’s events are made possible entirely through the dedication of volunteers and the generosity of our donors. All contributions are tax-deductible.
