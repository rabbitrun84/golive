Live music.

GoLive Musician’s League is a nonprofit building Silicon Valley’s live music culture.

We support local musicians, jam sessions, and emerging bands in Silicon Valley.

Born as a Saturday-night jam among friends, we now open stages across the Bay Area for singers, players and music lovers who would rather be on stage than just watch.
